# Cite & license

## Citing OEDI-SI

If you use OEDI-SI in your work, please cite it. The canonical metadata lives in
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/openEDI/oedisi/blob/main/CITATION.cff) in the
repository root.

### BibTeX

```bibtex
@software{oedisi,
  title   = {OEDI-SI},
  author  = {McKinsey, Joseph and Elgindy, Tarek and Latif, Aadil and Yang, Rui},
  organization = {National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR)},
  url     = {https://github.com/openEDI/oedisi},
  version = {3.0.1},
  year    = {2023}
}
```

### Plain text

> McKinsey, J., Elgindy, T., Latif, A., & Yang, R. *OEDI-SI* (Version 3.0.1) [Computer
> software]. National Laboratory of the Rockies. <https://github.com/openEDI/oedisi>
